01 · IT Services

When is VPS better than public cloud?

Public cloud is great for variable workloads but expensive for steady ones. An internal-facing application with relatively constant load, an MSSQL or MySQL database, or an internal app server — sometimes a dedicated VPS is more cost-effective, more predictable, and simpler. We help you make the right call.

02 · IT Services

OS management that doesn't fall through the cracks

An unmanaged server is a server that will eventually crash. Updates nobody installs, logs nobody reads, storage that fills up unnoticed. We're responsible for daily care — patches, scheduled reboots, log cleanup, and firmware updates — coordinated in advance for a proper service window.

03 · IT Services

Backup that's proven to work

Untested backup isn't backup. We perform a quarterly restore test, verify that RTO and RPO meet targets, and execute a structured DR plan in case of incident. When something happens, you don't panic — there's a clear protocol.

04 · IT Services

Monitoring with a monthly report

Once a month you get a report — uptime, performance, handled events, top five risks, and recommended actions. Without the report there's no real accountability. With it, we and the leadership team are aligned on the state of things.
